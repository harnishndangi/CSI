ABOUT US *

OUR
VISION AND
MISSION.

The Computer Society of India (CSI) Student Chapter at our college is a student-run organization dedicated to fostering technological excellence, creativity, and community.

Founded with a vision to bridge the gap between academia and industry, we empower students to grow as technologists, leaders, and innovators.

INNOVATE * COLLABORATE * BUILD * LEARN * GROW * LEAD * INNOVATE * COLLABORATE * BUILD * LEARN * GROW * LEAD *

OUR VISION

To be the premier student-driven technology community that shapes the next generation of innovators, problem-solvers, and tech leaders in our region.

OUR MISSION

To provide a platform for students to explore, experiment, and excel in computer science through hands-on events, mentorship, and meaningful projects.

OUR VALUES

Innovation. Inclusivity. Integrity. We believe every student has the potential to contribute meaningfully to the world of technology.

ABOUT CSI INDIA

PART OF A
BIGGER NETWORK.

The Computer Society of India is the first and largest body of computer professionals in India, founded in 1965. As an affiliated student chapter, we connect our members to a national network of 100,000+ professionals, industry resources, and opportunities.
